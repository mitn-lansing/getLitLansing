What is Get Lit Lansing?

Get Lit Lansing is where Mid-Michigan’s tech community gathers to show what they’ve built. Established teams, new startups, students, and solo makers share the same stage for a few minutes each.

The night is part show-and-tell, part hallway conversation. You will see live demos, meet the people behind them, and leave knowing more about what is happening in your own backyard.

Community showcase

Researchers, companies, garage tinkerers — one evening, one room.

Show your work

Five to seven minutes. A real thing. Honest feedback from people who build.

Meet your neighbors

Enterprise teams and solo makers in the same conversation.

Open to all

No pitch contest. No gatekeeping. If you built it and you are proud of it, bring it.

Why you should be there

You will be surprised by what is already being built in Lansing. Come watch it, then stay and talk to the people who made it.

See what is actually happening

Live demos from local companies, startups, and personal projects — automotive, health, education, and whatever someone finished last week.

Sharing, not competing

This is not a pitch-off. Companies trade notes with startups. Experienced builders talk with people presenting for the first time.

A scene that is growing

MSU talent, a thickening startup mix, and established tech teams. Be in the room while it is still easy to know everyone.

Useful introductions

The person next to you might be a collaborator, a hire, a mentor, or just someone who wants to talk about the same hard problem.

Your night to show it

Keeping a project in a private repo is safe. Showing it to people who care is more useful — and more fun.

No barriers

Free to attend. Open to every skill level and background. Enthusiasm for making things is the only requirement.

How the evening runs

Lightning demos, then time to talk. Speaker names land once the lineup is set.

  1. 4:30 PM

    Doors and mixing

    Come in, grab a drink, and wander the room. Demo stations are open before the program starts.

  2. 5:25 PM

    Welcome

    A short open, then we start the first block of demos.

  3. 5:30 PM

    Lightning demos

    Five to seven minutes each. Built things only — products, tools, research, games, hardware.

  4. Mid-evening

    Questions and a break

    Ask the presenters what you want to know, then get another look at the stations.

  5. Second block

    More lightning demos

    Another set of short shows, then closing remarks and a group photo.

  6. 7:30 PM

    Goodnight

    We wrap around 7:30. Stay a few extra minutes if you can help stack chairs.

Want to present?

If you have something you built and want seven minutes in front of this room, we want to hear from you. Companies, startups, students, and solo makers all get the same slot.
